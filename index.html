01

Quick-thinking warm-up

10 minutes · Speak for 30–45 seconds per question.

For every answer, include your opinion + a reason + an example. Avoid one-sentence answers.

1. Do people today have more opportunities than people did 50 years ago?
2. Does success depend more on talent or determination?
3. Can travelling genuinely change a person's personality?
4. Should young people prioritise education over personal interests?
5. Is it possible to be successful without taking risks?
Follow-up challenge

After each answer, challenge yourself: Could you think of an exception? What would someone who disagrees say? Would your opinion change in different circumstances? What might be the long-term consequences?

02

Speaking lab: defend your position

20 minutes · Choose a position, build an argument and respond to a counterargument.

Statement 1

Failure is an essential part of success.

Can people become successful without experiencing failure?

Statement 2

Modern technology makes people less independent.

Does technology develop our abilities or make us dependent?

Statement 3

Protecting historical buildings is more important than constructing modern ones.

Should cities prioritise preservation or development?

Statement 4

People should choose a career based on interests rather than salary.

Is job satisfaction more important than financial stability?

Your speaking structure

  1. State your position clearly.
  2. Give your first argument and explain it.
  3. Provide a specific example.
  4. Introduce an opposing viewpoint.
  5. Refute it or partly accept it.
  6. Draw a conclusion.

Devil's Advocate

Your teacher will argue the opposite. Respond without repeating your first argument.

ChallengeYour response

Vocabulary upgrade

10 minutes · Replace vague language with precise academic expressions.

Rewrite the highlighted idea using the expression bank. You may need to change the grammar of the sentence.

Open the expression bank

have a significant impact on · take measures to · extremely challenging · provide valuable insight into · be in decline · play a crucial role in · introduce stricter regulations · broaden one's horizons

Build complex sentences

Combine each pair using the structure in brackets.

1. People travel abroad. They learn about different cultures. (not only … but also)
2. The expedition was dangerous. The researchers refused to abandon it. (although)
3. Forests are disappearing. Many species are losing their habitats. (as a result)
4. Some people prefer online learning. Others prefer traditional classrooms. (whereas)
5. The team had prepared for months. They still faced unexpected difficulties. (despite the fact that)

Comparison challenge

1. 34% prefer shopping malls; 32% prefer online shops. Start: The proportion of respondents who…
2. Only 7% choose corner shops, while 34% prefer shopping malls. Start: Corner shops appear to be…

05

Essay workshop

25 minutes · Build three strong paragraphs before writing the full essay.

Writing task

Imagine that you are doing a project on shopping habits. You have found some data on the subject — the results of a survey conducted among adult Zetlanders.

Comment on the survey data and give your opinion on the subject of the project.

Paragraph 1 · Introduction + data overview

Target: 40–50 words. Paraphrase the topic and introduce the survey.

Paragraph 2 · Comparisons and comments

Target: 50–60 words. Include 2–3 accurate facts and at least one comparison.

Paragraph 3 · Problem and solution

Target: 50–60 words. Explain what the problem is, why it happens and how it could be addressed.

Conclusion · Your opinion

Language bank

Introducing: The survey was conducted to find out…

Reporting: According to the survey results…

Comparing: A slightly higher proportion… / Whereas…

Commenting: This may indicate that…

Problem: One potential drawback is…

Solution: This issue could be addressed by…

Conclusion: Taking everything into consideration…

Advanced requirements
  • Do not use I think more than once.
  • Include at least two complex grammatical structures.
  • Use at least four linking expressions.
  • Do not repeat people more than twice.
  • Explain what the figures suggest; do not merely list them.
  • Do not invent motives that the survey does not report.
06

Editing lab

10 minutes · Find the errors, correct them and explain the rule.

Read the paragraph carefully. There are several grammar and vocabulary problems.

Nowadays, online shopping become increasingly popular. One of the main reason is that it allow people to save time. However, there are also some disadvantages which should be take into consideration. For example, customers sometimes spend more money than they planned because they are influence by advertisements. Despite online shopping is convenient, traditional shops still play an important role. In my opinion, people should be more responsible when they make purchasing decisions.

Self-check: questions to ask yourself
  • Does the subject agree with the verb?
  • Is the noun singular or plural?
  • Do we need the passive voice?
  • Does despite take a noun or -ing form, rather than a full clause?
  • Is the collocation natural?
✓

Before you finish

A final quality check for your speaking and writing.

Speaking

Did I explain my ideas, give examples and consider another point of view?

Vocabulary

Did I choose precise expressions instead of simply using difficult words?

Essay

Did I report facts accurately, compare figures and develop the problem and solution?

Accuracy

Did I check verb forms, agreement, articles and collocations?

A convincing answer is not the longest one. It is the one in which every point is clear, relevant and supported.
